# Business Model

EarnSpace combines social identity, creator tools, Personal Spaces, content publishing, advertising, subscriptions, fan support, and future brand partnerships. Free social participation is the acquisition layer; creator and business capabilities are account-type extensions, not separate authentication systems.

Current code includes creator monetization, advertising, referrals, fan support, campaigns, withdrawals, and subscription models. Billing-provider checkout, tax handling, and paid entitlements require external payment configuration before launch claims are made.

Financial reporting must distinguish gross revenue, creator liability, withdrawal liability, platform revenue, fees, reversals, and net revenue. Historical ledger entries are append-only.
